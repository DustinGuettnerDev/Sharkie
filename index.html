In this game, you must guide your shark to the final boss and defeat him. Every enemy has its own weakness, so you need to figure out which attack works best against each one. You can avoid the regular enemies, but to complete the game, you must defeat the final boss.

Tipp: For every 7 coins you get hp back. If you have full hp you can collect only 6 coins.
